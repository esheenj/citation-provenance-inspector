      03 / THE PROVENANCE BOUNDARY

      A reference is
      not a verdict.

      This workbench borrows the idea of derivation and attribution from provenance modeling, but does not implement W3C PROV-O itself. It cannot establish whether a webpage exists, who authored it, whether a quotation is authentic, or whether a passage entails a claim. External sources, contradictory evidence and real-world citation quality still need human verification.
